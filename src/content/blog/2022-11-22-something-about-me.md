Hello! If you're reading this, you've stumbled upon my little corner of the internet where I share my thoughts, introspections, and occasional ramblings. Let me tell you a bit about who I am.

## On Solitude

I enjoy spending time alone. There's something deeply fulfilling about quiet space where I can think, read, or just exist without the need for constant interaction. Don't get me wrong—I value meaningful conversations with close friends—but my ideal weekend often involves solitude, a good book, or a documentary that makes me think. I'm also pretty private and tend to keep my personal life... well, personal.

## The Self-Critical Observer

I spend a lot of time thinking. And I mean a lot. I think about things, then I think about my thinking, and sometimes I even think about thinking about thinking. It's recursive and exhausting, but also kind of fascinating. This constant self-analysis means I'm incredibly self-critical—my own toughest critic, really. It's both a blessing and a curse. On one hand, this introspection helps me grow and understand myself better. On the other hand, I rarely feel confident about things, except maybe when I've had time to genuinely think something through for myself.

## The Beautiful Contradiction

Now, here's where things get interesting (and a bit messy). I'm a walking contradiction. I don't really have firm stances on most things, even when it might seem like I do. I'm perpetually indecisive. Every thought that crosses my mind feels like profound wisdom—at least in the moment! 🤣

It's like my brain is constantly hosting a debate club where every argument sounds equally convincing. Some might call this open-mindedness; I call it organized chaos.

## Why This Blog Exists

I consider myself a student of self-observation. This blog is my attempt to:

- **Document my journey** of understanding myself and the world around me
- **Share thoughts and opinions** on things that genuinely interest me
- **Connect with others** who might resonate with these ideas
- **Challenge my own thinking** by putting it out there

I'll try to keep things thoughtful and genuine, but I can't promise I won't go off on tangents. Actually, let's be real—tangents are where the interesting stuff usually happens anyway.

## What to Expect

This is where I work through ideas. You'll find personal reflections, random thoughts, and maybe the occasional technical note from my day job working with machine learning. Some posts will be polished; others will be raw thoughts still taking shape. All of them will be honest.

If you enjoy observing someone navigate the messy process of self-discovery, stick around. I hope you find something here that resonates, interests, or at least entertains you.

Thanks for being here. 🍺

— Prakash
